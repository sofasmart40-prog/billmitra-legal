Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how BillMitra handles information when you use the BillMitra billing app. “BillMitra”, “we” and “us” refer to the BillMitra app publisher. Questions or privacy requests: sofasmart40@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, BillMitra can store your business name, address, phone number, UPI ID, GSTIN, logo or signature; products, prices, stock, HSN codes, batch and expiry details; invoices, estimates, payments and customer names, addresses and phone numbers; purchase/expense details; and app settings. An optional owner PIN and recovery-code verifier are stored on the device in hashed form. Do not enter information you do not have a right to use.

Where information is stored

  • On your device: Business records are saved locally so billing can work offline. Clearing app/browser storage or uninstalling the app can remove local records. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Supabase cloud sync: When the app is configured for sync and has connectivity, it creates an anonymous app installation identity and syncs business records to the BillMitra Supabase project. This is a per-install backup, not an account-based recovery service; reinstalling or changing devices does not automatically restore it. Cloud data may remain after uninstalling.
  • Manual backup: If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose. You control that file and any further copy or sharing of it.

Optional third-party services

  • Subscriptions: On supported native apps, RevenueCat checks subscription entitlements. Google Play or Apple processes the purchase and payment. BillMitra does not receive your full card or payment-account credentials through this feature. Each provider processes information under its own privacy terms.
  • Gemini AI (Pro Premium): If you request sales insights, aggregate billed/collected/outstanding totals, bill counts and monthly totals are sent to the configured server and Google Gemini. If you request a payment reminder, the selected customer name, amount due, due days, invoice number and language are sent. If you scan a supplier invoice, the selected image is sent for extraction. These requests are optional; review AI output because it may be inaccurate. Do not submit information unless you are authorised to do so. Google processes submitted data under the terms applicable to the Gemini API; BillMitra does not control Google's retention or processing.
  • WhatsApp: When you choose a WhatsApp action, BillMitra opens WhatsApp with the message or bill details you chose to share. WhatsApp then processes the information under its own terms. You review and send the message; BillMitra does not send it in the background.
  • UPI: Payment QR codes are generated on your device from the merchant UPI ID and bill amount. Scanning the code opens the customer's chosen UPI app. BillMitra does not collect or process the resulting payment.
  • Read aloud: Reminder text is passed to the device's speech-synthesis facility; BillMitra does not create or upload an audio recording.

How we use information

Information is used to create and manage bills, estimates, inventory and customer balances; provide the sync, backup, subscription and optional AI features you request; and troubleshoot service problems. BillMitra does not sell your business records and the app does not display third-party advertising.

Retention, access and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or clear app storage. Exported files remain under your control. Synced cloud records can persist independently of your device. To ask about access or deletion of data held in the BillMitra Supabase project, email sofasmart40@gmail.com from the contact email associated with your request. We may need reasonable information to identify the installation and will handle requests subject to applicable law and technical limits. Deleting the app alone does not delete an already synced copy. For purchase records, contact the relevant app store; for WhatsApp or Google account data, use those providers' controls.

Security and children's privacy

Use a device lock and keep exported backups secure. Online transmissions use the configured service providers; no internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. BillMitra is a business utility and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Contact: sofasmart40@gmail.com.
