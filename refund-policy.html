Refunds & Subscriptions

Effective date: 8 October 2026

BillMitra Premium purchases are made through the app store shown at checkout. BillMitra does not collect subscription payments directly through the web version.

Cancel or manage a subscription

Subscriptions may renew automatically unless cancelled. To cancel, change payment details or check the next renewal date, use the subscription settings of the Google Play or Apple account used for purchase. Removing the app does not necessarily cancel an active subscription. Cancellation normally stops a future renewal; access generally continues for the paid period, subject to the store's rules.

Request a refund

Because Google Play or Apple processes the transaction, submit refund requests to the store that charged you. The store decides eligibility and timing under its refund rules and applicable law:

  • Google Play refund help
  • Apple: report a problem or request a refund

Contact sofasmart40@gmail.com if you need help identifying the BillMitra plan or resolving an app-access issue. Include the store order/reference number if you have it, but never email passwords, full card numbers or one-time codes. This support contact does not replace the store's refund process.

Nothing in this notice limits any consumer rights or refund rights that apply under local law or the store's terms.

Plan details

Lite Premium removes the Lite invoice watermark. Pro Premium removes the Pro invoice watermark and enables the Pro AI features described in the app. The store checkout screen displays the price, billing period and renewal terms that apply to your purchase. Store product availability and prices may vary by country and platform.
